VirtualHospitalAdvisor

A decision-support tool for Virtual Hospital investment selection

What this tool does

This assessment walks your organization through a structured, three-step process to answer two questions:

  1. Should we invest in a Virtual Hospital?
  2. If yes, which Virtual Hospital domain fits our organization — Care Delivery, Digital Twin, Workforce Simulation, or Immersive Training?
1

Identify & Prioritize Pains

Select your organization's active pains and rank them by severity, urgency, and frequency.

2

Assess Readiness

Rate your clinical, technology, workforce, and financial readiness.

3

Get a Recommendation

Receive a rule-based recommendation with a downloadable report.

Runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves your device. Local MVP, not yet clinically validated.
